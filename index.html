Technexus Myanmar ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Vehicle Maintenance Fleet mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may process, why it is processed, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

Account and Authentication Information

If you create or use an account, the App may process information required for authentication, such as your email address and authentication identifiers. The App supports email/password authentication and Google Sign-In.

Vehicle and Fleet Information

The App can store information that you enter about vehicles and fleet operations, including vehicle details, maintenance records, fuel records, documents, dates, mileage, notes, and related status information.

Backup and Export Information

When you use backup, restore, export, or file-related features, the App may access files or folders that you select on your device. These files are used to perform the requested backup, restore, or export operation.

Notification Information

The App may store notification-related information so that maintenance and document reminders can be scheduled and displayed. Notifications are used to provide reminders and other App-related messages.

Advertising and Device Information

When advertising is enabled, the App uses Unity LevelPlay mediation to request and display advertisements, including banner and rewarded advertisements. Advertising providers may process device and advertising-related information as required to provide, measure, protect, and improve advertising services. The availability of advertisements may also depend on network conditions, region, and advertising inventory.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the App may be used to:

  • create and authenticate user accounts;
  • save and synchronize vehicle and fleet records;
  • provide maintenance, document, fuel, and related features;
  • schedule and deliver reminders and notifications;
  • provide backup, restore, and export functionality;
  • maintain and improve App functionality and reliability;
  • provide advertising when advertising is enabled; and
  • respond to support and privacy requests.

3. Cloud Services and Third Parties

The App uses third-party services to provide parts of its functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

  • Firebase Authentication: account authentication.
  • Google Sign-In: optional Google account authentication.
  • Firebase Firestore: cloud storage and synchronization of App data.
  • Firebase Remote Config: remote App configuration, including the advertising feature switch.
  • Unity LevelPlay: advertising mediation and advertisement delivery when ads are enabled.

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared with service providers only as necessary to provide the functionality described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, protect the App and its users, or otherwise operate the service.

4. Local Storage

Some App information and settings may be stored locally on your device. Local storage is used to support App operation, preferences, cached data, notifications, and offline or device-level functionality.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled by the App. Data transmitted to supported cloud services is handled through the security mechanisms provided by those services. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and App data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain the App, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or enforce applicable agreements.

If you want to request deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact us at technexusmyanmar@gmail.com using the subject line Account Deletion Request. We will verify the request as necessary and process deletion of data that we control, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, or legitimate operational reasons.

7. Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to use Google Sign-In or email/password authentication.
  • You can control notification permissions through your device settings.
  • You can contact us to ask about your personal data or request deletion.
  • You can choose whether to use backup, restore, and export features.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general vehicle and fleet management use and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of using the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. When material changes are made, we will update the effective date on this page and may provide additional notice through the App where appropriate.
